# Retrospective

Read this file before writing the handoff.

List the ticket's misses, each once, from what the run already holds; never re-run a step to find more:

- on re-entry, the red publication gate, developer feedback, or red CI the change caused that opened the round, as a finding no review of the handed-off head raised;
- a finding the critique rejected that the developer later had applied.

Give each miss its cause, one of unclear ticket or specification, missing context, missing or misjudged check, or repeated manual step, and the smallest durable change it suggests, naming the skill, playbook, or ticket that change would edit. When the miss belongs to the work itself, say `no system gap`. Propose only: the retrospective edits nothing and publishes nothing. With no misses, omit the retrospective.
